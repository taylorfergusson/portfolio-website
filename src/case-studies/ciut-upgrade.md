## Overview

### Making a university radio station's website a reliable digital hub

CIUT 89.5 FM is the University of Toronto's campus and community radio station, with a website that had trouble connecting listeners, volunteers, and students to the station.

<div class="columns">

#### The Challenge

Identify the problems within CIUT's website content and structure that prevent listeners, students, and community members from meaningfully connecting with the station.

#### The Outcome

A concept redesign that revamps CIUT's website into a centralized hub for listening, discovering shows, attending events, donating, and volunteering, while still reflecting the fun, cool, and uninhibited personality of community radio.

</div>

## Problem

### I wanted to volunteer… but how?

CIUT is a volunteer-powered, student-and-listener-supported campus radio station for the University of Toronto.

…which is why I was so surprised that very little volunteer information was available on their website when I was looking to volunteer.

It didn't stop there. Valuable info on the organization, what it does, and how to engage with it was often missing, misplaced, or buried under noise.

For a nonprofit community radio station that runs primarily on donations and volunteer participation, its website needs to help make that happen.

> **Problem Statement**
> CIUT listeners and community members need a clear and reliable way to discover the station's information, media, and opportunities so that they can easily connect with and support the station.

## Research: Secondary

### What did I start with?

While this started as a content audit school assignment, I chose CIUT and took my work past the scope of the assignment since I had real stakes in the organization.

The audit was conducted in two phases, leveraging technical tools to get a head start before requiring qualitative judgment.

- ![](/images/ciut/icon-seo-crawl.png) **Phase 1: SEO crawl** I ran the site through Screaming Frog SEO Spider to extract all pages, files, images, metadata, links, etc. This mapped the site's internal ecosystem and revealed hidden technical issues.
- ![](/images/ciut/icon-manual-audit.png) **Phase 2: Manual content audit** I assessed the site components in depth, rating content from 1–5 based on whether it was:

<!-- This comment keeps the two groups of cards separate -->

- **Accurate** Correct and up to date
- **Actionable** Tells you what to do next
- **Findable** Easy to locate
- **Relevant** Serves the audience
- **Usable** Easy to understand

#### What I discovered

- ![](/images/ciut/icon-quality.png) **Low content quality** Broken links, inconsistent styles and layouts
- ![](/images/ciut/icon-context.png) **Missing context** Content not obviously relevant, missing "why"
- ![](/images/ciut/icon-stale.png) **Stale & incomplete content** Outdated info, no social media or event links
- ![](/images/ciut/icon-structure.png) **Poor structure & SEO** Large text blocks, metadata redundancy

The website failed to act as a central hub for what the station has to offer. Visitors have to put in more work than necessary to find information and piece it all together. The main culprits: inconsistency and incompleteness across the whole experience.

## Planning & Prototyping

### What should be done about it?

- Fix broken and outdated content
- Add context and clarity to all sections
- Restructure for readability and consistency
- Improve SEO (metadata + hierarchy)
- Link to social media and external content

## Competitive Analysis

### How do other stations do it?

I explored and analyzed the sites for CJTM (TMU's campus radio station), CKUT (McGill's campus radio station), and N10.as (a Montreal community internet radio station), which share similarities with CIUT but do have slightly stronger digital experiences.

Key patterns:

- "Listen Live" and "Donate" buttons always there, always clickable
- Clear layouts with clear structures
- Consistent show pages (styling, layout, information availability)
- Integrated events and external links (properly hubbified)

## Final Prototype

### CIUT.fm, revised

A prototype that:

- Serves all key information on a silver platter
- Uses consistent page structures and styles
- Improves navigation and readability (so many more skimming opportunities)
- Integrates social and external content (the world is your oyster)
- Adds a little bit more fun in the process

Key changes:

- Listening and donating is possible on every page
- Homepage gives a real-time peek into the current show and previews other site sections
- Dedicated "News & Events" section
- Structured show pages with consistent layouts

The redesign transforms the site into a clear, usable hub that:

- Makes it easier to get involved
- Improves content discoverability
- Better supports a modern audience

## Reflection

### At first, I knew the site felt difficult, but not why.

Breaking it down showed that the main issues were:

- Lack of structure
- Missing context
- Inconsistent content

This project helped me understand how to make content:

- Easier to scan
- Easier to understand
- More effective overall

It also showed how important it is to design for both new and existing users.
